Profile Guidance & Viva Tips

  • Academic Baseline: Minimum CGPA and graduation year limits are evaluated by the Rule-Based Gatekeeper before recommendation ranking.
  • Canonical Alias Resolution: Skills like postgres or sklearn are automatically mapped to PostgreSQL and Scikit-Learn.
  • Absence vs Inability: Missing skills represent absence from your profile, not proof of inability.
  • Persistent Storage: Profiles are stored directly in SQLite using parameterized queries with foreign key constraints.
